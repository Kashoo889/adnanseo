## Core Anti-Cannibalization Principles

1. **Service Pages Own Commercial Terms:** Clusters 1–11 map to commercial service pages. Never publish a blog post targeting a commercial head term like *"furniture removal dubai"*.
2. **One Area Page Per Community:** Clusters 15–17 (48 local terms) belong strictly to dedicated `/service-areas/[community]` pages.
3. **Blogs Own Questions, How-Tos, Cost, and "Free":** Clusters 12–14, 18, 20 (plus long-tails inside service clusters) form the blog content engine. Each blog article links down to relevant service or community area pages.
4. **Capture "Free" Honestly:** Turn high-volume "free removal" searches into paying customers through transparent explainers on municipality rules, charity donation criteria, and the risks of unlicensed scavengers.
5. **Featured Snippet Optimization:** Answer question keywords (`Q`) directly in a concise 40–55 word introductory paragraph under an `H2`, then expand with actionable steps.
